What's in the valley

Kinstead draws a real 3D town as crisp late-90s pixel art. Everything in it is made in code: every house is grown from a seed into a shape of its own, and every villager has a name, a home, a job and a routine.

villaStarter towns

Found a village in an empty valley, or take over one that's a few weeks along: a riverside hamlet, a market village, a mill town, a crossroads village, the Cathedral city, ten years on with some 3,000 villagers round a domed Capitol, or the Metropolis: the most people a valley can hold, some 47,000 of them round the Houses of Parliament.

holiday_villageNo two alike

Every building grows from a seed into a shape of its own. Two family houses side by side may be an L with a cross wing and a narrow house with a Dutch gable to the street; shops differ in storeys, roofs, shopfronts and signs; chapels, schools and farms set out their towers and barns their own way. Each keeps its shape as it grows.

scienceA research tree

Dozens of projects in branches from building works to transport unlock buildings, upgrade paths, building styles and new abilities: joining neighbours, buying land, moving houses, 2×2 estates, grand parks, a Capitol and a Parliament, and burying the power lines.

join_innerJoins and estates

Knock two neighbours into one: two cottages make a terrace, a home and a shop a shophouse, a tavern and a workshop a brewhouse. Grow a double plot onto the two beside it and rebuild it as a manor estate, a cathedral close or a college quad.

account_balanceThe seat of government

Build the town hall with a clock tower, a dome, in Gothic or behind a temple front, then grow it across bigger grounds into a Capitol and the Houses of Parliament. Law courts, a museum, an opera house and a library beside it make a civic quarter that raises every tax.

agricultureFarms and fields

Plough fields out beyond the farmyard and choose what each farm grows: wheat, barley or vegetables, or sheep, cattle and poultry on fenced pasture. The crops ripen and fall back to stubble with the seasons.

Farms and fields →
storefrontThe high street

A shop for every trade, from the greengrocer to the bank. Shopfronts turn the corner, the backs show the trade, and shoppers go to the nearer shops that sell what they're after.

The high street →
gondola_liftGondolas over the rooftops

Alongside the coaches and steam trains, string a gondola between stations on stilts. Its cabins run on a cable straight from station to station, over streets, roofs and the river, and its pylons stand just tall enough to clear what lies below. Villagers ride whenever it beats the walk.

diversity_3A living town

Every villager is a little minion in blue dungarees, with one goggle or two and a touch of their trade, and has a name, an age, a family, a home, friends and a trait or two. Children go to school, grown-ups to work or college, shoppers carry home their baskets, and families move in when the town is a happy place to live.

People →
nightlightDay and night

A day passes in eight minutes at normal speed. Lamps come on once the town has power, villagers carry lanterns home, and a soundtrack made up as it plays slows into a minor key at night.
